\section{Ongoing Research}

\subsection{Modeling of clustered censored survival data with spatial correlated random effects using SBART (under review at Biometrics)}
We present a Bayesian survival model to discover important factors contributing to racial disparity among patients in Florida with breast cancers. We propose a general robust nonparametric model for clustered censored survival data with spatial correlation under a Bayesian ensemble learning paradigm called Soft Bayesian Additive Regression Trees or SBART (Linero and Yang, 2018).

\subsection{Estimating Causal Effects in Spatially Clustered Survival Data Using BART (ongoing)}
We propose a novel Bayesian approach to estimate causal effects in spatially clustered survival data. Using Monotone Bayesian Additive Regression Trees (mBART), we introduce a flexible framework that accommodates unobserved cluster-level covariates and spatial associations through a Conditional Auto-Regressive (CAR) model. Additionally, we incorporate a propensity score model to estimate treatment effects in observational studies.

\subsection{Bayesian extension of "Flexible multivariate linear mixed models for structured multiple traits"}
I worked on extending the paper "Flexible multivariate linear mixed models for structured multiple traits" using Bayesian approaches.

\subsection{Bayesian Tensor regression on a big skewed data with mixed response}
We analyze dental data with a mixed tensor response. The main challenges are monotonous missingness, large and longitudinal data, with each patient being recorded at irregular time points. We are developing a method/model that can tackle these challenges.

\section{Academic Projects}

\subsection{Fraud Detection in Highly Imbalanced Big Data}
\textit{Bank Of Malaysia}\\
We provided a solution to a classification problem where we were tasked with Detecting Fraud in highly imbalanced data where the number of zeroes was much higher than ones, leading to simple classification algorithms predicting zero almost always and giving very low precision on test data, thus, failing to serve the task. We checked the linear dependency of predictors, which helped us argue why statistical techniques based on linear dependence wouldn't be sensible. We finally came up with a series of methods that can be used to classify the data with high accuracy. We showed that t-SNE's results on the data were reliable, and our algorithm consisted of taking a Nadaraya-Watson estimate based on the distance with fixed k nearest neighboring data points. We used the k-fold cross-validation and compared the performance with that of SMOTE.

\subsection{Implementation of Projection Pursuit}
\textit{supervised by Prof. Soumendu Sundar Mukherjee}\\
We implemented Projection Pursuit on three types of problems - Regression, Density Estimation, and Exploratory Data Analysis. We projected our data in one dimension, took the density of the projection, optimized Friedman's index using Gradient Descent, updated the model, and iterated it until convergence.

\subsection{Public Perception on Inflation in our Area}
\textit{supervised by Prof. Debasis Sengupta \& Prof. Bimal K. Roy}\\
We devised a tool to measure inflation in our area and found out the most influencing factor on wholesale price index \& most influencing factor that hinders the customer's purchasing power. We prepared questionnaires and surveyed a stratified sample. We defined an index, named the customer price index, that depends on the time point. We derived quantitative indices that explain the perception of inflation: Jack Knife estimates, ratio estimates, and minimizer estimates. Then, we did a reliability analysis of the data. We used various plots to explain the behavior of the data and why certain types of analysis will be suitable.

\subsection{Estimation of the Mean of a Multivariate Normal Distribution-A Review}
\textit{supervised by Prof. Avik Ghosh}\\
Estimation of the means of independent standard random variables using the squared error loss function is considered. An unbiased estimate of risk is obtained for an arbitrary class of estimates. A significant type of minimax estimate is found when the dimension is at least 3. Lastly, approximate confidence sets for the mean vector centered on an arbitrary estimate are given.

\subsection{Analysis of Air Pollution Data-Big Data Analysis}
\textit{supervised by Prof. Debasis Sengupta}\\
Handling highly Big Data. Different analyses like Anova, Prediction of different variables, Explaining behaviors of different variables, Analysing interesting features of the data using various Multivariate Techniques.

\subsection{Radio Colouring of Graph Theory}
\textit{supervised by Prof. Sandip Das}\\
We tried to find a bound for a minimum number of colors required to radio color some special types of infinite graphs. We found a strong upper bound that we conjectured also to be the lower bound, hence claiming that the upper bound we got might be the actual chromatic number in any such graph.
